# Mercurio design

Mercurio wears the Ormus fusion style: a midnight lacquer surface, gold as the only accent, a serif display face over Inter and JetBrains Mono. The brand has no light mode, and neither does the app. The brand is defined in Ormus Solutions' private ormus-brand repository (`STYLE.md`, `tokens.json`); this app consumes a vendored copy of its tokens and defines no brand color of its own.

## Where the values come from

| Layer | File | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Brand tokens (vendored) | `app/brand/ormus-tokens.json` | Copy of `ormus-brand/tokens.json` v0.4.0 (commit 47b5c20) |
| Kotlin tokens | `ui/theme/OrmusTokens.kt` | Generated by `./gradlew :app:generateOrmusTokens` |
| XML tokens | `res/values/ormus_tokens.xml` | Generated by the same task (window background, status bar, launcher icon) |
| Material 3 scheme (dark only, one per planet) | `ui/theme/Theme.kt`, `ui/theme/Color.kt`, `ui/theme/Planet.kt` | A planet palette from the tokens, plus tones derived by compositing alpha tokens the way the web renders them |
| Extras (status, hairlines, spacing, motion) | `ui/theme/Ormus.kt` | Tokens; read with `Ormus.extras`, `Ormus.spacing`, `Ormus.motion` |
| Type | `ui/theme/Typography.kt`, `res/font/` | Brand families, bundled |
| Terminal palette | `data/ColorSchemePresets.kt` (`Ormus Fusion`) | Tokens for black, red, yellow, white, bright black, bright yellow, bright white; the other hues are proposed for an ormus-brand `terminal` group |
| Shared pieces | `ui/components/OrmusComponents.kt` | `OrmusCard`, `OrmusGroup`, `OrmusBubble`, `StatusDot`, `StatusChip`, `MonoLabel`, `Modifier.ormusPanelTop()` |

Refresh after a brand change: copy the new `tokens.json` over `app/brand/ormus-tokens.json`, run `./gradlew :app:generateOrmusTokens`, commit both outputs. `OrmusThemeTest` fails when the generated Kotlin drifts from the vendored JSON.

## Modes

- Dark only. STYLE.md says "There is no light mode", so the app renders a dark scheme whatever the phone's system setting is.
- Planet themes. The settings panel (the console's gear) picks one of the seven classical planets from the tokens' `planets` group (`ui/theme/Planet.kt`). Each planet keeps every role and swaps the palette: its accent takes the gold slot, so "gold is the one filled action" reads "the planet's accent is the one filled action". Sun equals the `color` group and is the default. A pick repaints the app at once (`rememberPlanet`). The default terminal scheme, Ormus Fusion, is drawn in the planet too, and open sessions repaint on a pick (`ColorSchemeRepository`, `TerminalBridge.refreshPlanetColors`); a profile on another scheme keeps its colors.
- The earlier light ("daylight") scheme and its Settings choice were removed. A theme saved by an older version, light included, is deleted at launch (`AppViewModel`), so an upgrade lands on dark with nothing else changed.
- Accents. The `accents` group (rose, teal, leaf, ember, sky, iris; `OrmusTokens.Accents`) marks what a thing is, such as a space tile or an author. An accent never carries state alone and stands for no planet. Gold stays the primary accent: one gold element per view. Every accent reads at 4.5:1 on every surface container of every planet (`OrmusContrastTest`).
- The brand's print group (`OrmusTokens.Print`) stays in the generated tokens; the dark scheme borrows only print gold, for `inversePrimary`.

## Status colors

State reads through intensity, and every state also has a shape, so color never carries it alone.

| State | Color | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| blocked | gold | filled |
| done, connected, online | ink | filled |
| working | ink muted | ring |
| idle, closed, offline | dim | hollow ring |
| connecting | gold bright | filled |
| error, dropped | danger lifted 25% toward ink | filled |

`Ormus.extras.status` exposes these.

## Type

| Role | Family | Use |
|---|---|---|
| headline*, titleLarge | Cormorant Garamond 300/400 | top bars, prompt titles. Never bold. |
| title*, body*, labelLarge | Inter 400/500/600 | names, body, buttons |
| labelMedium, labelSmall | JetBrains Mono 400/500, 0.08em | `/ SECTION` labels, chips, keycaps, endpoints, session output |

Fonts are bundled unmodified from upstream static builds (Cormorant v4.002, Inter v4.1, JetBrains Mono v2.304) under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows bundling inside an app. None declares a Reserved Font Name. License texts ship in `assets/licenses/fonts/`.

## Shape, spacing, elevation, motion

- A radius scale of three values. `radius` 8dp for keys, buttons and fields (`OrmusCornerShape`). `radiusContainer` 14dp for cards, groups, sheets and message bubbles (`OrmusContainerShape`). `radiusPill` 999dp for chips and tags (`OrmusPillShape`). Material's two small sizes take 8dp; medium, large and extra large take 14dp (`OrmusShapes`). Status dots and switch thumbs stay round.
- Container rule: a list of one kind of thing sits in one container (`OrmusGroup`): a mono `/ SECTION` label above it, the container at the container radius, rows split by hairlines inset 16dp from the start. Rows keep their own click handling and padding. One group per kind of thing, never a group inside a group.
- Bubble rule: a message sits in a container by author (`OrmusBubble`). YOU: right side, surfaceContainerHighest with a 16 percent gold tint (a tint, so ASK stays the one gold container), tail corner at the bottom end. AGENT: left side, surfaceContainer with a hairline, tail corner at the bottom start. TOOL: a quiet mono line (labelMedium is applied by the bubble) on surfaceContainerLowest at the key radius, full row width. ASK: the pinned question, primaryContainer with a gold border, the one gold container in the view. YOU, AGENT and ASK take at most 88 percent of the row and shrink to their content. An optional identity accent colors the author label; YOU is never labeled and ASK ignores it (an accent on the gold tint reads 4.41:1 on the Moon planet). Text reads at 4.5:1 on every author tone (`OrmusContrastTest`).
- Spacing on a 4dp grid: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32. Touch targets 48dp minimum; key rows stay at 46dp height with full row width.
- Flat surfaces. Depth comes from the surface-container family and hairlines; cards lift 2dp only while pressed.
- Motion uses the brand reveal curve `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`: 150-200ms for state changes, 1100ms reserved for first reveals.

## Contrast

`OrmusContrastTest` checks the scheme (WCAG 2.x): onSurface, onSurfaceVariant, primary text and error text at 4.5:1 or better on every surface container; outline and status marks at 3:1. One pinned exception: the dark `error` role is the danger token itself (4.22:1 on deep), so error text in Mercurio's screens uses `Ormus.extras.status.error` (5.72:1 on deep).

## Launcher icon

The flat Ormus gold mark (`ormus-brand/assets/marks/ormus-mark-gold-1024.png`), traced with potrace to four filled vector paths, on a midnight-to-deep background with the gold bloom at top right. Adaptive foreground, background and monochrome layers are VectorDrawables; the legacy PNGs and the notification silhouette are scaled down from the 1024 mark, never up.
